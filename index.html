Flip to Focus

Turn your phone face-down to focus. Pick it up and the session pauses. Every pause is recorded so you can see how you really study.

  1. Tap Start and allow motion access.
  2. Keep this page open in Safari.
  3. Lay the phone face-down on a flat surface.

Only motion data is used, only while this page is open. Your records stay on this phone until you export them.
